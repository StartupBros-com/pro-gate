## Highlights

- A review whose caller died, or that nothing is waiting to collect, no longer keeps a review slot busy for hours.
- Queued reviews stop timing out behind those abandoned reviews, while recent reviews keep their slots exactly as before.
- The abandoned review is still saved and can be collected later at no cost; nothing is deleted or charged again.

## Upgrade

Update the Pro-gate plugin and its matching runtime. Nothing else to do. To keep the previous behavior, set `PRO_GATE_GENERATION_CEILING=0`.

## Details

A reservation still marked as generating used to hold an account slot until the 6-hour TTL and three confirmed marker misses removed it. A caller that died, or one that parked on a capture it could not bind to its run, left nothing to collect the review, so its slot stayed taken. Two such reservations held half the account for hours while queued reviews timed out (#273).

A generating reservation now stops counting against capacity 3 hours after its charge (`PRO_GATE_GENERATION_CEILING`, default `10800`). That is far beyond any measured generation. Owner probes saw a completed answer within 592 seconds of send (30 observations). 1,817 successful fresh runs finished within 3,838 seconds of taking their slot. The longest conversation ever seen still writing, at 9,432 seconds, never produced a review.

The check runs only when capacity is read. The reservation, its charge, its harvest command and its removal rule are unchanged. A reservation without a state, with an unknown state, or with a missing, invalid or future clock keeps holding. The slot plan, the holding count, the slot-wait holder report and `--status` all use the same rule. `--status --json` adds `past_generation_ceiling` and the state `past-generation-ceiling`.
