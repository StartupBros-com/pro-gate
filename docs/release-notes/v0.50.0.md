# v0.50.0

## Highlights

- A review that ends by asking you to choose between two options no longer becomes unanswerable because of a blank line in the answer.

## Upgrade

Nothing to do — the next review picks it up automatically. A review already sitting in this state
becomes answerable again on the next check, without paying for a new one.

## Details

When a review cannot settle a question on its own, it ends by offering a small set of named
choices and asks a person to pick one. Pro-gate reads those choices out of the answer and presents
exactly them, and nothing else, as the decision to make.

That reader treated any line after the choices as the start of unrelated prose and stopped,
returning no choices at all. An empty line counted. Models routinely put one between the last
choice and the closing verdict line, which is ordinary formatting and still satisfies what the
review was asked to produce. When that happened, a completed review that had asked a clear
question came back with an empty set of options, and the only thing pro-gate could report was that
the answer was unreadable — for a review that was already paid for, and whose text was sitting
right there.

- **Blank lines between the choices, or before the closing verdict, are now read as formatting.**
  They carry no instruction, so they cannot begin a second block, and the choices around them are
  returned as written.
- **Everything else about the reader is unchanged.** A line with actual content after the choice
  block still ends the parse, as do duplicate option names, malformed lines, oversized text, and
  control characters. The verdict itself is untouched: a review that asked a question still asks
  it, and nothing is upgraded or accepted on the strength of this change.
