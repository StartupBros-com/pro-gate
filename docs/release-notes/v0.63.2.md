## Highlights

- Recognize grounded review citations to dot-prefixed paths, including `.claude-plugin/marketplace.json:5-19`.
- Preserve compatibility with retained review vocabulary and the existing normalized citation forms.

## Upgrade

Update the Pro-gate plugin and its matching runtime. This patch does not resume stopped reviews or change their budgets.

## Details

The tokenizer removed the leading dot from a rationale citation but retained it in suffixes derived from Git diff paths. Even after 0.63.1 fixed line-range parsing, an exact citation to a hidden directory could therefore be rejected as `template-verdict`.

The shared tokenizer now recognizes that path form without replacing the established normalization. A citation still has to match the retained reviewed vocabulary. Unknown paths, partial names, malformed ranges and references appearing only on the verdict line do not establish a grounded rationale. Review ownership, provenance, charges and recovery limits are unchanged.
